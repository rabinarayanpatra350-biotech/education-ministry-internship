# Education Ministry Internship — Government Systems & Procedures

**Week 1 Task** | Education Ministry Internship, Coimbatore | Rabi Narayan Patra

A research report on how the Government of India administers education, from the constitutional framework down to the Coimbatore district office, built entirely on verified official data (Union Budget 2025-26, UDISE+ 2023-24, PIB releases, Lok Sabha answers, and Tamil Nadu Government Orders).

## What the report covers

| Section | Focus | Key verified figures |
|---------|-------|------------------------|
| 2. Organizational structure | Union → State → District hierarchy | MoE budget 2025-26: **₹1,28,650 crore**; Dept. of School Education ₹78,572 cr; Higher Education ₹50,078 cr |
| 3. Administrative procedures | Business rules, file noting, e-Office, RTI, grievances | RTI Act 2005: 30-day reply; Coimbatore CEO office as district RTI authority |
| 4. Acts & regulations | RTE Act, NEP 2020, UGC/AICTE Acts | RTE Section 12(1)(c): 25% EWS quota; NEP 6%-of-GDP target vs 4.64% actual (2020-21) |
| 5. Budgeting & finance | Budget cycle, demands for grants, MUSK | Samagra Shiksha ₹41,250 cr; PM POSHAN ₹12,500 cr; PM SHRI ₹7,500 cr (BE 2025-26) |
| 6. Schemes & programs | Central + Tamil Nadu schemes | 13,092 PM SHRI schools selected; TN Breakfast Scheme: 20.59 lakh students; Illam Thedi Kalvi: ~2 lakh volunteers |
| 7. Inter-departmental coordination | Convergence mechanisms | 18,610 schools with NSQF vocational education; anganwadi–UDISE+ data boundaries |
| 8–9. Limitations & future work | Honest self-critique + concrete next steps | — |

## Scale of the system (UDISE+ 2023-24)

- **14,71,891** recognized schools (10,17,660 government)
- **24.8 crore** students enrolled (pre-primary to higher secondary)
- **98.08 lakh** teachers
